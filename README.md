Customer & Sales Insights – Diwali Festive Season EDA

📊 A comprehensive Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) project on Diwali Sales Data using Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, and Seaborn. This project uncovers customer purchasing behavior, top-performing product categories, high-revenue states, and key demographic segments to support data-driven business decisions during India's festive shopping season.

Buyer Persona Development
Business Recommendations & Insights

Major Findings
Married women aged 26–35 are the highest-value customer segment.
Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra, and Karnataka generate the highest sales.
IT, Healthcare, and Aviation professionals contribute significantly to revenue.
Food, Clothing, and Electronics are the top-performing product categories.

Project Objective
To analyze Diwali sales data and identify customer behavior patterns, profitable market segments, and business opportunities that can improve marketing strategies, inventory planning, and customer targeting.
